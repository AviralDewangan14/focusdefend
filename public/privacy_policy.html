Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 16, 2026

🔒 Core Privacy Commitment

FocusDefend processes app restriction logic, focus timers, and quiz answers locally on your device. We enforce a strict Zero Keylogging policy, zero screen scraping, and never sell personal data to advertisers.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the essential information necessary to provide our digital wellbeing and EdTech learning services:

  • Account Data: Email address, encrypted authentication tokens (via Supabase Auth), and optional phone number.
  • Profile Information: User role (Student, Parent, Teacher), grade/class (Classes 6–12), academic stream, and target competitive exams.
  • Study & Focus Metrics: Focus session duration, streak count, quiz scores, accuracy metrics, and Focus Credits balance.
  • App Restriction Preferences: Package names of applications designated for focus restrictions by the user or linked parent.

2. Prominent Disclosure on Android Permissions

In accordance with Google Play Developer Program Policies, FocusDefend provides explicit disclosures regarding Android permissions:

📊 Usage Access (PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS)

FocusDefend uses Android's official UsageStatsManager API to detect when selected restricted apps enter the foreground during study hours and to track active screen time against user-configured daily limits.

  • No Accessibility API: FocusDefend does NOT use the Accessibility API, does NOT inspect UI node trees, and does NOT record window contents.
  • Zero Keystroke Logging: We never log or record typed text or passwords.
  • Local Real-Time Processing: Foreground app detection is evaluated strictly on-device in real-time memory and is never transmitted to external servers.

Display Over Other Apps (SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW)

Used to display the curriculum challenge barrier, focus countdowns, and the interactive study gate over apps designated for focus protection.

Battery Optimization Exemption

Ensures scheduled study blocks and timers are not interrupted by aggressive OEM task killers.

3. How We Use Information

  • Deliver scheduled focus protection and distraction blocking.
  • Generate adaptive NCERT practice questions and track revision progress.
  • Award Focus Credits and calculate study streaks.
  • Synchronize study analytics between linked Parent and Student dashboards.

4. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, trade, or rent personal data. Information is only shared between linked family accounts or with trusted cloud infrastructure providers (Supabase) under strict data protection and encryption agreements.

5. Children's Privacy & Parental Consent (COPPA & DPDP Act 2023)

FocusDefend is built strictly as an educational and parental-guard companion. Under both the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act):

  • Verifiable Parental Consent: Processing of student data is conducted strictly with verifiable consent from the linked parent or legal guardian.
  • No Tracking or Targeted Advertising: In compliance with Section 9 of the DPDP Act, FocusDefend does not engage in tracking, behavioral monitoring for advertising, or targeted advertisements directed at minors.
  • No Detrimental Processing: No data processing is undertaken that could have an adverse or detrimental effect on the physical, mental, or educational wellbeing of any child.
  • Parental Rights: Linked parents retain absolute authority to inspect, correct, export, or permanently erase all profile records and study metrics.

6. India Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act Compliance

As a Data Fiduciary under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDPA), FocusDefend adheres to the highest standards of data governance for users in India:

  • Notice & Lawful Grounds (Section 5): Clear itemized notices are provided detailing precisely what data is collected (grade, subject, quiz responses) and for what purpose (delivering curriculum practice and screen-time defense).
  • Purpose Limitation & Minimization (Section 6): We collect solely the minimum data necessary for educational operation. Real-time app inspection occurs 100% on-device and is never transmitted off-device.
  • Right to Access & Information (Section 11): Users have the right to request a summary of personal data being processed and identities of processing entities.
  • Right to Correction & Erasure (Section 12): Users can review, update, or erase inaccurate data or request complete account erasure directly from the in-app settings or by contacting our Grievance Redressal Officer.
  • Right of Grievance Redressal (Section 13): Users can file privacy grievances directly with our designated Grievance Officer, with guaranteed resolution within 7 working days.
  • Right to Nominate (Section 14): Data Principals have the right to nominate an individual to exercise their rights in event of incapacity.

7. Data Security & Storage

All data in transit is encrypted using TLS 1.3 / HTTPS, and stored data is encrypted using AES-256. Users can request immediate and permanent deletion of their account by emailing privacy@focusdefend.com or via the in-app Settings menu.
